# 🧁 Yummy Dolce — Your Online Menu

Dear Yummy Dolce,

We made this for you — a little gift to help your business grow. 💛

Your customers can now see your full menu on their phone, any time, anywhere.
No app to download, no account to make — just a link you share with them.

**How it works for your customers:**

- They open the link and see your whole menu
- They pick what they want (sizes, flavors, extras — just like ordering in person)
- They tap **Order**, and their order comes straight to you on WhatsApp

That's it. Simple for them, easy for you.

---

## What's yours to use

- **Your menu link** — share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or print it on a card. Your customers will find everything they need.
- **Your order number** — every order lands in your WhatsApp, exactly as your customer set it up.

## Making changes (whenever you want)

You have your own little office for the menu — open it at:

**`your-site-address/admin.html`**

- Log in with the passcode you were given
- **Add or edit items** — new sweets, new prices, new photos
- **Upload pictures** of your treats (they're automatically made nice and small, so your menu loads fast)
- **See your orders** — every order that comes in is saved there, so nothing is ever missed

Change anything, save it, and your customers see the update right away. It's all online — you never have to wait for anyone to make changes for you.
